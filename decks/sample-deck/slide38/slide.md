The **default** preset is the only theme. It uses **Libre Baskerville** for headings, **Libre Franklin** for body text, and the navy / slate / coral palette (`#1A1A2E`, `#4E4E7E`, `#E94560`, `#F0F0F0`, `#0A0A0A`).

Use per-slide **`slide:`** overrides in frontmatter to tweak fonts or colors when needed.
